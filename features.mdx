## Quote shopping

Quote shopping helps you compare multiple market options without manually tracking every carrier separately. A strong quoting workflow usually includes intake, carrier matching, follow-up questions, and a final recommendation.

### Example quote payload

<CodeGroup tabs="JavaScript,cURL">
  ```javascript
  const quoteRequest = {
    accountName: "Starline Logistics LLC",
    dotNumber: "3141592",
    fleetType: "regional trucking",
    vehicles: 18,
    drivers: 22,
    effectiveDate: "2026-01-01",
    requestedCoverages: ["auto liability", "cargo", "physical damage"]
  };

  const response = await fetch("https://api.example.com/quotes", {
    method: "POST",
    headers: {
      "Content-Type": "application/json",
      "Authorization": "Bearer YOUR_API_TOKEN"
    },
    body: JSON.stringify(quoteRequest)
  });
  ```
  ```bash
  curl -X POST "https://api.example.com/quotes" \
    -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
    -H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_TOKEN" \
    -d '{
      "accountName": "Starline Logistics LLC",
      "dotNumber": "3141592",
      "fleetType": "regional trucking",
      "vehicles": 18,
      "drivers": 22,
      "effectiveDate": "2026-01-01",
      "requestedCoverages": ["auto liability", "cargo", "physical damage"]
    }'
  ```
</CodeGroup>

### What to look for

- Whether the requested coverages match your risk profile
- Which carriers return competitive pricing
- Whether underwriting conditions add extra work later
- How quickly you can move from intake to recommendation

## Named advisor communication

Named advisors give you a direct line for questions, document follow-up, and time-sensitive requests. This reduces back-and-forth and helps you avoid repeating the same context across multiple conversations.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Email" icon="mail">

    ```text
    Subject: Renewal documents for the 2026 term

    Hi Jordan,

    I have attached the updated driver roster and loss runs.
    Please confirm whether you need any additional documents before carrier review.

    Thanks,
    Alex
    ```

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Text" icon="message-circle">

  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Documents and COIs

Document handling keeps certificates, policy forms, and renewal files easy to find when you need them. That matters when a shipper asks for proof of coverage, when a carrier needs a signed form, or when your team is preparing renewal materials.

### Common document types

| Document type | Typical use |
| --- | --- |
| COI | Proof of coverage for shippers, brokers, or vendors |
| Declarations page | Summary of policy terms and coverages |
| Loss runs | Historical claims data used in underwriting |
| Renewal packet | Supporting materials for the next policy term |

<Callout kind="tip">
  Keep document names consistent so your team can find the latest version quickly during renewal season.
</Callout>

## Safety and renewal insights

Safety insights help you understand fleet risk over time, not just at the point of sale. Use operational data to spot trends early, then bring those trends into renewal planning.

### Example safety dashboard data

```json
{
  "fleetName": "Starline Logistics LLC",
  "scoreDate": "2026-01-15",
  "scores": {
    "driving": 91,
    "hoursOfService": 88,
    "fitness": 94,
    "csa": 96,
    "vehicle": 90,
    "sureScore": 92
  },
  "alerts": [
    "New hard-braking trend detected on route 12",
    "Driver assignment review recommended for two units"
  ]
}
```

### How you might use the data

1. Review alerts weekly instead of waiting for renewal.
2. Share notable trends with your advisor before carrier review.
3. Track improvements over time so you can show progress at renewal.
4. Escalate recurring issues that may affect pricing or eligibility.

## Claims and renewal workflows

Claims history and renewal prep are closely connected. When you understand what happened during the term, you can present a clearer story to carriers and reduce avoidable surprises.

<Expandable title="What to include in a renewal package" default-open="true">
  
- Current policy documents
- Updated vehicle and driver schedules
- Recent claims summary
- Safety improvements and training updates
- Any changes to routes, freight, or operations
  
</Expandable>

<Expandable title="How to handle a new claim" default-open="false">
  
- Log the claim as soon as it is reported
- Capture date, location, vehicle, and involved parties
- Notify your advisor if the loss may affect renewal timing
- Keep supporting documents in one place for follow-up
  
</Expandable>
