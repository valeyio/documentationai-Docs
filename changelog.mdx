## How to Read This Page

### What to include

A strong changelog entry tells readers what changed, who may be affected, and whether they need to take action. Keep each update focused on the outcome, not just the implementation.

### What to avoid

Do not overload the page with unrelated notes. If a change affects setup, migration, or support, call that out directly so users know what to do next.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review the release" icon="book-open">

    Read the update title, date, and version first so you can understand the scope of the change.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Check impact" icon="shield">

    Look for setup changes, workflow updates, or behavior changes that may affect your team.

  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply actions" icon="check-circle">

    Follow any required steps, then confirm the update works as expected in your environment.

  </Step>
</Steps>
