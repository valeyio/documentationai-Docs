## Getting Started

- Go to `https://dashboard.pullsure.com`
- Click **Sign Up** and complete the registration
- Verify your email
- Log in and create your first project
- Use the sidebar to navigate your docs

Your workspace lets you invite team members and manage documentation easily. Pullsure helps you keep your project documentation organized and secure for better renewals.
