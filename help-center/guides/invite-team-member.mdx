---
title: How to Invite a Team Member
description: Quick guide to adding teammates to your pullsure workspace.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Team Settings">
    In the pullsure dashboard, click your workspace name > Team Settings.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Invite Member'">
    Enter your teammate's email address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send Invite">
    Click **Send Invite**. They'll receive an email to join your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Manage team">
    Monitor invites and view all members in the Team Settings page.
  </Step>
</Steps>
